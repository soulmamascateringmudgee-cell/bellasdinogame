Pick a voice

Tap a voice to hear it. My voice plays your own recordings. Starred device voices sound most natural.
iPad/iPhone tip: download a better one in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → Australian → Karen → Enhanced or Premium, then reopen the game.

Record my voice

Tap ● Record, read the line out loud in your best dino-mummy voice, tap ■ Stop. Tap ▶ to check it. Lines you skip use the device voice.
Important: recordings are saved where you record them. On an iPad, the Home Screen icon and Safari are separate, so record in the one Bella plays in.
